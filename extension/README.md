# FiatWallet Browser Extension

Self-custodial Solana Web3 Browser Extension that injects `window.solana`, `window.fiatwallet`, and registers with the **Solana Wallet Standard**.

---

## Features
• **Universal dApp Compatibility:** Injects `window.solana` and registers with the official Solana Wallet Standard.  
• **Auto Detection on Top Platforms:** Meteora, Jupiter, Raydium, Tensor, Magic Eden, Pump.fun, and any Solana dApp automatically recognize FiatWallet.  
• **Secure Approval Prompts:** Prompts the user to approve connections and transaction signing.  
• **Direct Balance Checking:** View your real-time SOL balance and copy your address directly from your browser toolbar.

---

## How to Install in Chrome, Brave, or Edge (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. In the address bar, go to:
   • Chrome: `chrome://extensions`  
   • Brave: `brave://extensions`  
   • Edge: `edge://extensions`  
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button (top-left).
5. Select the `extension` folder inside your FiatWallet project:
   `C:\Users\DELL 7400 2IN1\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\fiatwallet\extension`
6. Click **Select Folder**.

FiatWallet will appear in your browser extension toolbar!

## How to Use on Meteora, Jupiter, & dApps

1. Click the FiatWallet icon in your browser extensions bar.
2. Enter your wallet address (e.g. your FiatWallet address).
3. Open any Solana dApp (e.g. `https://app.meteora.ag` or `https://jup.ag`).
4. Click **Connect Wallet** on the dApp.
5. FiatWallet will appear in the wallet list and prompt you to connect!
