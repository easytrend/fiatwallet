2. Problem & Market Opportunity (Why Nigeria, Why Now)

The Pain Points in Existing P2P

  • The Bank Freeze Epidemic (PND): Informal P2P platforms (Binance P2P, Bybit, Telegram OTC) route funds through unvetted personal accounts. A single flagged payment cascades into Post-No-Debit (PND) account freezes by commercial banks.
  • The "Gas Trap" & High Drop-off: Non-technical users receive stablecoins but cannot move or cash them out because they do not own native SOL to pay blockchain transaction fees.
  • Cryptographic Complexity: 44-character base58 Solana addresses and manual 10-digit account number entries lead to costly human error and user anxiety.

Market Drivers

  • Global Leader in Adoption: Nigeria ranks #2 globally in crypto adoption (Chainalysis) and leads the world in peer-to-peer transaction volume.
  • The Flight to Digital Dollars: With local currency depreciation, stablecoins (USDC/USDT) have transitioned from speculative trading assets to essential tools for wealth preservation, cross-border commerce, and remittances ($50B+ annual sub-Saharan volume).
  • The Regulatory Shift: Regulatory bodies (including SEC Nigeria's VASP and Accelerated Regulatory Incubation Programme - ARIP) are establishing frameworks that favor transparent, compliant, KYC-verified fintech rails over shadow P2P.

3. Core Product Suite & Architecture

  • Instant Automated P2P Gateway: Sub-45 second automated settlement for buying and selling stablecoins via domestic bank transfers, verified by on-chain Solscan receipts.
  • Sponsored Gasless Relayer: Serverless relayer co-signs and sponsors network fees so users can transact seamlessly even with 0 SOL in their wallet.
  • Universal Multi-Chain Ingestion (NEAR Intents): Direct native deposits from Bitcoin (BTC), Tron (TRC-20 USDT/TRX), and EVM chains (Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Ethereum, BNB) straight into Solana with zero wrapped-token friction.
  • Human-Readable FiatTags ($tag): Handles like $fiatwallet or $ultradefi eliminate long public keys and 10-digit account numbers.
  • Camera OCR Scanner: Computer vision scanner reads printed or written 10-digit bank account numbers in one click.
  • Flexible Onboarding: Connect existing wallets (Phantom, Solflare, Mobile Wallet Adapter), create a new 12-word seed phrase, import an existing vault, or explore via Guest Room.
  • Solana Rent Reclaim: Built-in tool audits closed SPL token accounts to recover dormant SOL rent fees back to the user.

5. Competitive Advantage (The Moat)

  1. Compliance as a Shield: Direct BVN/NIN identity verification and verified banking rails completely eliminate account freezes and fraud that plague informal P2P.
  2. Web2 Simplicity on Web3 Rails: Human-readable $FiatTag handles, camera OCR scanner, 4 flexible onboarding modes, and gasless relayer allow seamless adoption by everyday non-crypto users.
  3. Multi-Chain Liquidity Ingestion: Frictionless deposits from Bitcoin, Tron, and EVM chains directly into Solana via NEAR Intents chain abstraction.
  4. Strictly Non-Custodial Integrity: Private keys and seed phrases remain encrypted locally on client devices (AES-256 vault) and never touch centralized servers.

6. Regulatory & Banking Strategy

  • Licensed Partner Rails: Fiat transactions and domestic banking disbursements are executed via licensed payment gateway infrastructure (PajCash), ensuring strict AML/CFT compliance.
  • Regulatory Alignment: Architecture built to align with SEC Nigeria’s Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) and ARIP sandbox guidelines.
  • Segregated Risk Architecture: Commercial banks settle purely domestic fiat-to-fiat transactions without direct crypto balance exposure, insulating users and banking partners from regulatory friction.
