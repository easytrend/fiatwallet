FiatWallet ("we", "us", "our") is an onchain peer-to-peer (P2P) platform that enables users to buy and sell stablecoins on the Solana blockchain. FiatWallet is accessible at fiatwallet.app and as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

FiatWallet provides the following services:

  • P2P buying and selling of stablecoins via PajCash and PayPal
